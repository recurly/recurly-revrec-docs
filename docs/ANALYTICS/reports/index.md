### Prerequisites

<ul class="rp-list">
  <li>Familiarity with your organization's RevRec Essentials.</li>
  <li>Understanding of product bundling and data rule configurations.</li>
  <li>Knowledge of the desired attributes for dataset creation.</li>
</ul>

### Limitations

<ul class="rp-list">
  <li>Any changes you make apply to future transactions only.</li>
  <li>Changes to revenue data can take up to an hour to appear in reporting.</li>
  <li>Once a dataset is created, it can't be deleted — only inactivated.</li>
  <li>Data rules can't be deleted, but they can be set to an inactive status.</li>
</ul>

# Definition

<div class="rp-definition">Recurly RevRec's reporting module offers a wide range of detailed reports for your financial and contractual needs — from revenue insights to transaction details. Each report gives you a view into revenue recognition, contractual balances, costs, variable considerations, and more. Through an intuitive interface, you can configure reports, save layouts, and generate output tailored to your requirements.</div>

## How to save layouts in reports

Once you open a report, you can apply filters and adjust columns to create a custom format — called a layout — that you can save for future use.

<div class="rp-steps">
  <div class="rp-step">
    <div class="rp-step-num">1</div>
    <div><h4>Set up your report</h4><p>Open the report you want and fill in any search criteria.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="rp-step">
    <div class="rp-step-num">2</div>
    <div><h4>Customize the layout</h4><p>Select the Mapper, check the attributes you want to display, and drag them into the desired order.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="rp-step">
    <div class="rp-step-num">3</div>
    <div><h4>Generate the report</h4><p>Select Run to view the customized report.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="rp-step">
    <div class="rp-step-num">4</div>
    <div><h4>Save the layout</h4><p>Select Save Layout on the right-side bar, enter a unique name, and select Save.</p></div>
  </div>
</div>


<Image src="https://files.readme.io/66b63691b31bbf5ab33615a3c3c3bda57930285e8eb26a5a3b698a631548bc04-5._Save_Layout_-_Step_5.png" align="center" width="75%" border={true} />


<div class="rp-steps">
  <div class="rp-step">
    <div class="rp-step-num">5</div>
    <div><h4>Access saved layouts</h4><p>To view a saved layout, select the drop-down menu in the upper right corner next to Search — your saved layouts are listed there.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="rp-step">
    <div class="rp-step-num">6</div>
    <div><h4>Save additional layouts</h4><p>To create another layout, modify the filters or columns and select Save again. Toggle the save option to "No" when prompted, to save it as a new layout.</p></div>
  </div>
</div>


<Image src="https://files.readme.io/6cbb0aba2d81c66592747d50d9abe44002831fec96f8a395780dca11de4daa98-6._Save_Layout_-_Step_7.png" align="center" width="75%" border={true} />
