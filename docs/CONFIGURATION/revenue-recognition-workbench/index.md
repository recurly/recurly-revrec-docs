### Prerequisites

<ul class="rp-list">
  <li>A valid Recurly account with appropriate permissions.</li>
  <li>Basic understanding of Recurly's revenue recognition processes.</li>
  <li>Familiarity with contract terms and conditions.</li>
</ul>

### Limitations

<ul class="rp-list">
  <li>Any changes you make apply to future transactions only.</li>
  <li>Modifications must be approved by both parties.</li>
  <li>Not all types of modifications may be supported under certain conditions.</li>
  <li>Manual modifications using the Revenue Workbench may be treated differently.</li>
</ul>

# Definition

<div class="rp-definition">The Revenue Workbench is a comprehensive tool that provides detailed insights into contracts and their related elements. It lets you review performance obligations, variable consideration, billing information, costs, revenue waterfalls, journal entries, modifications, allocation details, contract balances, business events, holds, and audit trails — all in one place.</div>

# Manual action tools

To access manual action tools, right-click a field within the desired contract. The options available depend on the field selected.


<Image src="https://files.readme.io/fedc373-4.png" align="center" width="75%" />


## Linking and de-linking contracts

<div class="rp-steps">
  <div class="rp-step">
    <div class="rp-step-num">1</div>
    <div><h4>Select Link</h4><p>Right-click the desired field and choose "Link."</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="rp-step">
    <div class="rp-step-num">2</div>
    <div><h4>Choose the type</h4><p>In the Type tab of the Link dialog box, choose between creating a new contract or adding to an existing one.</p></div>
  </div>
</div>


<Image src="https://files.readme.io/1a77845-image.png" align="center" width="75%" />


**Create New Contract:** Select this option to create a new contract for the lines de-linked from the current contract. Add a comment for reference, then save.


<Image src="https://files.readme.io/7bcce0b-image.png" align="center" width="75%" />


**Add to Another Contract:** Select this option to link the de-linked lines to a different existing contract.


<Image src="https://files.readme.io/3fb8280-image.png" align="center" width="75%" />


<ul class="rp-list">
  <li><strong>Contract# field:</strong> Enter the contract number to which the selected lines should be linked.</li>
  <li><strong>Treatment:</strong> From the Treatment dropdown, choose either "Cumulative Catch up" or "Prospective" based on the desired revenue recognition approach.</li>
  <li><strong>Comments:</strong> Provide a brief explanation for the linkage in the Comment box to support documentation and tracking.</li>
</ul>

## Moving the POB

<div class="rp-steps">
  <div class="rp-step">
    <div class="rp-step-num">1</div>
    <div><h4>Select Move POB</h4><p>Right-click the desired field and choose "Move POB." A dialog box opens with the following options.</p></div>
  </div>
</div>

**New POB:** Select this option to assign a new POB to the selected lines, creating a new performance obligation specifically for them.


<Image src="https://files.readme.io/9cebc4d-image.png" align="center" width="75%" />


**Add to Existing POB:** Select this option to manually add the selected lines to an existing POB within the same contract, consolidating related lines under a single performance obligation.


<Image src="https://files.readme.io/5c30add-image.png" align="center" width="75%" />


<div class="rp-steps">
  <div class="rp-step">
    <div class="rp-step-num">2</div>
    <div><h4>Add comments</h4><p>Enter any relevant comments to explain the rationale for the POB movement or provide additional context.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="rp-step">
    <div class="rp-step-num">3</div>
    <div><h4>Save</h4><p>Select the Save icon to update the POB assignment for the chosen lines.</p></div>
  </div>
</div>

## Recognizing revenue

<div class="rp-steps">
  <div class="rp-step">
    <div class="rp-step-num">1</div>
    <div><h4>Select the lines</h4><p>Highlight the lines you want to manually recognize or defer, then right-click to access the available options.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="rp-step">
    <div class="rp-step-num">2</div>
    <div><h4>Select Recognize</h4><p>Choose "Recognize." A dialog box opens with the following options.</p></div>
  </div>
</div>


<Image src="https://files.readme.io/2983955-image.png" align="center" width="75%" />


<ul class="rp-list">
  <li><strong>Type:</strong> Choose the appropriate revenue type from the dropdown to categorize the revenue for recognition or deferral.</li>
  <li><strong>Action:</strong> Select either "Recognize" or "Defer" based on the desired treatment.</li>
  <li><strong>Percentage:</strong> Enter the percentage of revenue to recognize or defer for the selected lines.</li>
  <li><strong>Journal dates (optional):</strong> Enter a start and end date if you want the recognition or deferral to apply only to certain journal dates.</li>
  <li><strong>Comments:</strong> Provide a clear explanation for the chosen recognition or deferral to maintain proper documentation.</li>
</ul>

<div class="rp-steps">
  <div class="rp-step">
    <div class="rp-step-num">3</div>
    <div><h4>Save</h4><p>Select the Save icon to apply the recognition or deferral settings to the selected lines.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="rp-step">
    <div class="rp-step-num">4</div>
    <div><h4>Review the results</h4><p>Navigate to the Waterfall tab to see the revenue allocation and the impact of the recognition or deferral on the revenue waterfall.</p></div>
  </div>
</div>


<Image src="https://files.readme.io/23aa12d-image.png" align="center" width="75%" />

# FAQ

<Accordion title="What is the Workbench and how is it used to manage revenue recognition contracts?">
  The Workbench is the central place to review and manage revenue contracts and contract groups. From here, you can search and filter contracts, inspect line details and POB assignments, apply holds, and perform corrective actions (subject to permissions). For step-by-step use cases, see the Revenue Recognition Workbench documentation.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is it possible to change a POB after a contract has been processed?">
  Yes. RevRec lets you manually change a POB after it has been assigned. Use the Change POB action in the Workbench to reassign the performance obligation — the system recalculates schedules according to your rules and book settings. Be aware that changes can affect recognition timing and may create reallocation or restatement entries depending on period status and approvals. For detailed instructions, see Change POB in the Workbench docs.
</Accordion>
